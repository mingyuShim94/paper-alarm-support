Paper Alarm

A bedtime alarm with a handwritten to-do photo mission.

Privacy policy

Get started

Choose your bedtime and repeat nights, allow alarms, then practice taking a photo. Write the date shown in the app followed by three tasks, one per line. A successful photo check completes your bedtime mission.

If a photo does not pass

Write the date clearly. Keep one date and all three tasks inside the guide. Use good lighting and move the phone farther away if text is blurry. Handwriting recognition is not perfect. Retake your photo if needed. After repeated failed checks, the app offers an option to end without completing; this does not count toward your streak.

Alarms and morning reminders

Enable Paper Alarm's alarm permission in iPhone Settings. Repeat days refer to the bedtime night; the morning reminder is on the following day. The morning reminder is a notification, not a wake-up alarm. Paper Alarm does not block other apps or monitor sleep.

Ads and privacy choices

Ads may appear on the morning receipt. A rewarded ad is optional to restore an eligible broken streak. Bedtime writing and completion screens have no ads. Where available, use Ad privacy choices in Help & Privacy to review your consent. Ad availability varies and a failed ad request does not block writing or viewing your note.

Your photos

Recognition takes place on your iPhone. Saved note photos are cleared after 30 days when the app performs cleanup. New temporary captures are cleaned up after their flow ends or on the next launch after interruption. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; operating-system backups are managed separately by you.
