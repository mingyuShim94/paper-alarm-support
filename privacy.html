Privacy Policy

Paper Alarm · Updated September 29, 2026

Support

What stays on your device

Paper Alarm processes handwritten dates and tasks using Apple's on-device text recognition. We do not upload your camera images or handwritten note content to our servers or send that content in advertising requests. Alarm settings, completed-night records, and saved note photos are stored locally. You do not need an account.

Photo retention

Saved note photos are removed after 30 days when the app next runs its cleanup. Temporary captures and intermediate crops created by the updated app are removed when no longer needed in the capture flow, or at the next launch following an interruption. Previously created diagnostic files are not retroactively included in this cleanup. Uninstalling the app removes the app's local data. Any device backups are controlled by your operating-system settings.

Advertising and consent

Paper Alarm uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Ads are requested only after applicable consent checks allow ad requests. The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Non-personalized ads are not data-free: Google may process IP-derived approximate location, device or app-scoped identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash diagnostics, and performance information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and analytics.

Your note photos and task text are not included in these requests. Where required, a consent form is shown before advertising. You can revisit available consent choices through Help & Privacy → Ad privacy choices. If consent cannot be checked or ads are unavailable, the main app functions remain usable.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Permissions

Camera access is used for note photos. Alarm permission is used for bedtime alarms. Notification permission is optional for morning reminders. You can change these permissions in iPhone Settings.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide so we can respond. Avoid sharing sensitive task content or photos. We use support messages to address your request, not to personalize ads.
